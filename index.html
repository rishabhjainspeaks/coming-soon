Who is Rishabh Jain?

Rishabh Jain is an Indian Chartered Accountant (ICAI, November 2023) who works in treasury at HSBC in GIFT City, Gandhinagar. Before treasury, he spent nearly two years in Internal Audit at the Mahindra Group's Group Corporate Office in Mumbai, covering procure-to-pay, order-to-cash, supply chain, risk and compliance. Alongside his finance career, he is an empanelled ICAI Career Counsellor and Mentor, runs AI literacy seminars for chartered accountants, mentors CA students, and publishes content on AI, productivity and human behaviour in Hinglish under the name rishabhjainspeaks.

Is Rishabh Jain an ICAI career counsellor?

Yes. He is an empanelled ICAI Career Counsellor and Mentor. He conducts CA career counselling sessions for school students (Classes X–XII) and commerce undergraduates (B.Com, BBA), explaining the Chartered Accountancy course, its pathway and career options.

What AI seminars does Rishabh Jain conduct?

Rishabh Jain runs a practical AI literacy seminar series for chartered accountants, CA firms, ICAI branches, CA student associations, colleges and schools. The core session is a 75-minute, demo-heavy workshop showing how CAs can use AI tools safely in daily work — for example, verifying a sales sample and drafting a management email, spotting risky clauses in a loan document, and handling client confidentiality by stripping sensitive data before using AI. Sessions have been delivered for the ICAI Bikaner Branch, Kottayam SICASA (Southern India Chartered Accountants Students' Association) and the CA firm DMKH & Co.

He holds the AICAI Level 1 certification (a three-day AI course covering AI fundamentals, prompt engineering, vibe coding and automation, and robotic process automation), participated in the ICAI AI Committee's AI Hackathon Season 6 (2026), and teaches:

  • Fundamentals of generative AI for finance professionals
  • Prompt engineering for audit, tax, accounting and treasury work
  • Vibe coding: building small tools with AI without being a programmer
  • Automation and RPA for accounting workflows
  • Data confidentiality when using AI with client information
